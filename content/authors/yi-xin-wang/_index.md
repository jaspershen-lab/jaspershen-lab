Yi-Xin Wang (汪一心) is a Research Professor and doctoral supervisor at the School of Public Health, Shanghai Jiao Tong University School of Medicine. He serves as Vice Dean of the School of Public Health and Head of the Department of Environmental Health.

His research focuses on environmental and reproductive epidemiology across the life course. Combining environmental science, epidemiology, artificial intelligence and multi-omics, he investigates environmental risks to reproductive health and how reproductive factors influence the short- and long-term health of mothers and children.
