Seung-Min Park is an Assistant Professor in the School of Chemistry, Chemical Engineering and Biotechnology at Nanyang Technological University Singapore. With a background in physics and applied physics, his research brings together engineering and medicine to develop technologies for precision health.

His work spans micro- and nanotechnology for molecular diagnostics, liquid biopsy and the analysis of human excreta. He develops health-monitoring approaches, including smart-toilet technologies, to support continuous, non-invasive assessment of health.
