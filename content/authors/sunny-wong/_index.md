Sunny Wong is a clinician-scientist and Associate Professor of Nutrition, Digestion and Metabolism at the Lee Kong Chian School of Medicine, Nanyang Technological University Singapore. He serves as Programme Director of Microbiome Medicine and Assistant Dean (Academic Medicine).

His research examines how gut microorganisms interact with their human host in digestive and metabolic diseases, and how these insights can inform new biomarkers and therapies. His work also investigates the relationships between diet, the microbiome and metabolism.
