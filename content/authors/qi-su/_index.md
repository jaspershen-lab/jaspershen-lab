Qi Su is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Medicine and Therapeutics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong. His work combines molecular biology and bioinformatics to investigate the gut microbiome and its relationship to human diseases. He analyses large multi-omics datasets for clinical and research applications, with interests in gastrointestinal diseases, the gut–brain axis, autism and insomnia.
