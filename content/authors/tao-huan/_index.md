Tao Huan is an Associate Professor of Analytical and Bioanalytical Chemistry in the Department of Chemistry at The University of British Columbia. His research develops analytical and computational methods for mass spectrometry-based metabolomics and applies them to biological questions.

His team works to improve metabolome coverage and confidence in metabolite identification, integrate metabolomics with other omics data, and investigate disease mechanisms and biomarkers through systems biology.
