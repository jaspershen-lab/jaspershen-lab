abstract: "Liquid chromatography-tandem mass spectrometry untargeted metabolomics captures thousands of metabolic peaks from biological samples. Conventional identification-dependent metabolomics workflows require confident structural annotation before identified features can enter downstream pathway or functional analysis. However, only a small fraction of detected metabolic features can be assigned confident structural annotations, leaving a large fraction difficult to incorporate into downstream biological interpretation, a form of \"functional dark matter\". Here, we introduce Spec2Function, a function-centric framework that infers biological functions from tandem mass spectrometry (MS2) spectra. Spec2Function is trained using characterized spectrum-text pairs but does not require prior structural identification of the query spectra at inference time. At its core is Spec2Biotext, a dual-encoder contrastive model that embeds MS2 spectra and curated multi-aspect natural language functional texts into a unified functional space. Combined with a retrieval-augmented generation framework grounded in curated databases and biomedical literature, Spec2Function retrieves evidence-grounded biological knowledge and produces coherent functional summaries for individual spectra or groups of spectra. Across metabolite- and pathway-level benchmarks, Spec2Function achieved higher similarity to reference functional descriptions than the evaluated comparators and expanded the fraction of the metabolome accessible to functional interpretation. Application to a human liver cirrhosis cohort recapitulated known metabolic alterations and identified additional literature-supported functional modules. Applying Spec2Function to a 30-organ macaque aging atlas revealed organ-specific metabolic functions and previously unreported functional modules that were supported by independent human and mouse datasets. Together, these results position Spec2Function as a function-centric framework for scalable biological interpretation of structurally unannotated MS2 spectra."
