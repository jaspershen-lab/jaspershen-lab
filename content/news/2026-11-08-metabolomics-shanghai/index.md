Xiaotao SHEN and Yijiang LIU attended **Metabolomics 2026 in Shanghai**, sharing Shen Lab’s research through a talk and a poster presentation.

### Xiaotao SHEN — Research presentation

On November 8, Xiaotao presented **Deciphering the Biological Function of Tandem Mass Spectra Using a Dual-Modal Metabolite Language Model**, discussing the lab’s work on interpreting tandem mass spectra.
