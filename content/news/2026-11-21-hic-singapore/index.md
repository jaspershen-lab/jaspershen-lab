**Yuting WANG** and **Jiramet (Jay) KINCHAGAWAT** represented Shen Lab at the **Hyper Interdisciplinary Conference (HIC) Singapore 2026** on **November 21, 2026**. The conference brought together researchers and professionals to discuss artificial intelligence in medicine and the life sciences.

### Yuting WANG — FeatureMAP oral and poster presentations

Yuting shared **FeatureMAP: network-guided interpretation of untargeted metabolomics** in an oral presentation and a poster session. The presentation highlighted the use of network- and AI-guided analysis to make metabolic signals interpretable, followed by discussions with attendees at the poster.

Jay presented **scCody: distinguishing aging-aligned from divergent disease-associated cellular remodeling**. The poster session provided an opportunity to explain the work and exchange ideas with other conference participants.
