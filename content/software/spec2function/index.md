Spec2Function is a function-centric framework for interpreting tandem mass spectrometry (MS2) spectra without requiring prior structural identification. Its dual-modal model, Spec2Biotext, connects spectra and biological function descriptions in a shared embedding space. Retrieval-augmented generation draws on curated databases and biomedical literature to produce evidence-grounded functional summaries for individual spectra or groups of spectra.

The web app provides **Single Spectrum** and **Metabolite Set** workflows for exploring the biological functions of metabolomics data.
