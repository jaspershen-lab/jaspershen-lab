FeatureMSEA performs metabolite set enrichment directly from LC-MS metabolic features without requiring confident metabolite identification. It combines feature-level enrichment scoring, iterative annotation refinement, permutation-based statistical inference, and optional LLM-assisted interpretation to identify enriched metabolite sets.
